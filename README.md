# SmartBotIA

### Building intelligent AI assistants for a smarter future.

**SmartBotIA** is an AI initiative focused on creating intelligent assistants that support people, developers, professionals and organizations in their everyday work.

Our vision is to make AI more accessible, useful and practical through specialized assistants designed for real-world needs.

## Discover KEN IA

**KEN IA** is our AI assistant platform, designed to support different use cases:

### KEN IA Learn
AI-powered learning support, helping learners understand concepts, practice skills and progress at their own pace.

### KEN IA Developer
An AI assistant designed to support software development, technical problem-solving and developer productivity.

### KEN IA Business
An evolving direction focused on professional productivity, business assistance and everyday work processes.

### KEN IA Enterprise
Exploring intelligent AI solutions adapted to organizational needs and business environments.

## Our Vision

We believe AI should do more than answer questions.

It should help people:
- Learn and develop new skills.
- Solve technical and professional challenges.
- Work more efficiently.
- Access intelligent assistance adapted to their needs.

## Development

SmartBotIA is an evolving initiative. We are progressively developing, testing and improving our AI solutions.

Our public showcase presents the vision and progress of the project. Proprietary source code and internal infrastructure are maintained separately.
